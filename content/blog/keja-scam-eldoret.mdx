---
title: "How to know a keja is a scam in Eldoret"
description: "Red flags to check before sending a rental deposit in Eldoret."
---

A low price and a landlord who is always "out of town" can sound tempting. Pause before sending a deposit.

Meet at the actual house, confirm the caretaker can show the exact unit, and check that the utility meter details match the listing. Be wary of pressure to pay before a viewing or requests to send money to a third party.

A RentiSafe inspection is a point-in-time check, not a guarantee. Keep your own records and make the final decision yourself.
