---
title: "What to check on a KPLC meter before renting"
description: "Simple questions about the meter and utility account to ask before you rent."
---

Ask to see the meter for the unit you will occupy and compare its identifier with any details the landlord shared. Confirm whether electricity is prepaid or billed, and clarify who is responsible for outstanding balances.

Do not share tokens or account credentials with strangers. A meter match is only one part of a rental check; it does not prove ownership or guarantee a safe tenancy.
