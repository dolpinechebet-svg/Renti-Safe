---
title: "How to verify a caretaker before paying"
description: "Practical steps for checking a caretaker's authority at a rental property."
---

Ask to meet at the property, not in a car park or office. Request confirmation from the owner using a contact sourced independently, and never rely on an ID photo alone.

A verifier can check whether the caretaker on site matches the listing details. Identity documents are sensitive: do not circulate photos of IDs in public groups. RentiSafe stores evidence privately and recommends redacting ID numbers.
